krishiRakshak AI

AI-Powered District-Level Crop Disease Intelligence Network for Rice Farmers in Andhra Pradesh

KrishiRakshak AI is an AI-powered system designed to reduce preventable crop yield losses by providing early detection, district-level aggregation, predictive outbreak forecasting, and multilingual alerts for farmers and agricultural officers.

This platform is built to operate effectively in rural environments with low connectivity, supporting both text and voice advisories in Telugu, and leveraging serverless cloud architecture for scalability and cost-efficiency.

Problem Statement

Rice farmers in Andhra Pradesh face significant yield loss due to delayed detection of crop diseases and lack of community-level outbreak intelligence. Current systems are reactive, fragmented, and often fail to provide timely intervention at the district level. Agricultural officers lack real-time visibility into disease clusters and outbreak trends, which limits their ability to prioritize interventions effectively.

KrishiRakshak AI addresses these gaps by combining image-based disease detection, geo-tagged aggregation, predictive outbreak forecasting, and tiered alerting, creating a district-scale intelligence network that enables proactive intervention.

Core Features

Image-Based Disease Detection

Detects rice diseases such as blast, brown spot, and bacterial blight from farmer-uploaded images.

Community Disease Radar

Aggregates geo-tagged detection data to identify outbreak clusters at village and district levels.

District Crop Disease Risk Score

Computes a 0–100 risk score for districts based on detections, weather data, seasonal trends, and crop growth stages.

Predictive Outbreak Forecasting

Generates short-term predictive alerts to support preventive measures before disease spreads.

Tiered Smart Alert System

Delivers alerts based on risk levels (Low, Moderate, High) to farmers and officers via text and voice in Telugu.

Farmer Dashboard

Mobile-first, low-literacy-friendly interface displaying recent detections, risk scores, and preventive guidance.

Officer Intelligence Dashboard

Heatmaps, trend analytics, and priority alerts for agricultural officers to support district-level decision-making.

Offline & Low-Bandwidth Functionality

Supports offline operation, queued uploads, and optimized performance on 2G/3G networks.

Scalability and Cost-Efficiency

Serverless architecture capable of supporting 10,000+ concurrent users with automated scaling.

Data Security and Privacy

Encrypts all data, anonymizes farmers’ identities during aggregation, and complies with Indian data protection regulations.
